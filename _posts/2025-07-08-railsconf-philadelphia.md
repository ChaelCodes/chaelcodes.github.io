[RailsConf 2025](https://railsconf.org) was the 20th and final RailsConf, taking place at the Pennsylvania Convention Center in Philadelphia from July 8-10, 2025. This three-day conference brought together the Rails community for one last celebration of everything that makes Ruby on Rails special. It was bittersweet, energizing, and filled with incredible talks, hallway conversations, and Ruby friends!

### From Java to Rails: 500 Days of Flexcar's Migration
John Dewsnap

So cool to hear a follow-up on Flexcar which moved from microservices in Java to Rails! Rails was easier, faster, and overall a great choice. They kept their React frontend, frontend devs could work on backend changes, and the welcoming Rails community made the transition smooth. One key insight: "Complex features require smaller teams."

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/events/railsconf-2025)*

## Final Thoughts

RailsConf 2025 was an unforgettable experience. While it's bittersweet that this was the last RailsConf, the energy, community, and passion for Rails were stronger than ever. From the opening reflections to Aji's incredible closing keynote, every moment reminded me why I love this community.

The hallway track, hack spaces, dinners, and game nights were just as valuable as the talks themselves. Being able to connect with maintainers, contributors, old friends, and new faces made this conference truly special.

Thank you to Ruby Central for organizing 20 years of RailsConf, and to everyone who attended and made this final conference memorable. While RailsConf may be ending, the Rails community continues to thrive!

If you're interested in watching any of these talks, keep an eye on [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/events/railsconf-2025) where the recordings will be posted.
