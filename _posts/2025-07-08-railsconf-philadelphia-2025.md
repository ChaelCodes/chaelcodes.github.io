[RailsConf 2025](https://www.rubyevents.org/events/railsconf-2025) was the final RailsConf, hosted in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania on July 8-10, 2025. After years of bringing the Ruby and Rails community together, this was the last time we'd gather under the RailsConf banner. It was a bittersweet celebration of everything the conference has meant to our community, packed with amazing talks, hack spaces, and the kind of connections that make Ruby special.
