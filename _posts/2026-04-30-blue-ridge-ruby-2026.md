[Blue Ridge Ruby 2026](https://www.blueridgeruby.com) was a two-day Ruby conference in Asheville, North Carolina on April 30 and May 1, 2026, with a community hack day on May 2. It brought together Rubyists for thoughtful talks, practical ideas, and lots of community energy in the mountains.

## The Conference Experience

Blue Ridge Ruby felt warm and intentional from the first welcome remarks.
I especially loved the blend of deeply technical talks, people-focused talks, and hallway conversations that kept going long after sessions ended.

## The Talks

### Learning from Permaculture: Sustainable Software Development
John Athayde
> I've farmed with permaculture since 2013 and designed & built software for 20+ years. Turns out the same principles apply: observe before acting, produce no waste, value the margins. Let's explore what the garden can teach us about building software that lasts.

John connected software architecture to permaculture in a way that was practical and memorable.
"The domain is the typography" and "observe before acting" were two lines that really stuck with me.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/john-athayde-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

{% include gallery.html gallery=page.galleries.john_athayde_talk %}

### Yes, &…: Ruby's Secret Talent for Improvisation
Ifat Ribon
> Did you know Ruby is an improviser? With its ability to take blocks at will, Ruby lets methods pass the conversation to blocks that happily accept what's given and add to it. This talk explores blocks, procs, and lambdas as Ruby's way of saying "Yes, and…", with playful examples, surprising behavior, and joyful chaos along the way.

Ifat gave an incredible talk that made technical details of blocks, procs, and lambdas feel approachable and fun.
The improv framing made the whole thing click.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/ifat-ribon-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

{% include gallery.html gallery=page.galleries.ifat_ribon_talk %}

### How To Finish What You Start: Lessons From Actually Shipping a Big Refactor
Annie Kiley
> Every developer has at least one major refactor they would love to make happen. Somehow, these projects are tough to start and even tougher to finish. My team recently completed a major overhaul of our permissions and access system, and it felt great to actually get one of these big refactors done. In this talk, I'll share the practical lessons that helped us ship it without breaking things or losing momentum, so you can finish your next big change too.

This one was very relatable.
Annie's focus on planning how things ship, keeping work in small slices, and staying focused is the kind of advice that holds up in real projects.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/annie-kiley-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

{% include gallery.html gallery=page.galleries.annie_kiley_talk %}

### InstiLLMent of Successful Practices in an Agentic World
Kevin Murphy
> Congrats on joining Hours Unlimited. The Math and Numbers team is excited to have you join us on our journey to redefine the importance of numerals. This introductory session will provide tips and tricks for best interacting with powerfuLLMachine, the next-level platform we use to unlock productivity and effectiveness.

Kevin's talk was hilarious and sharp.
I laughed hard, and then it landed emotionally with a real reminder about people, teams, and where we're headed.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/kevin-murphy-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

### Terminus: A Hanami + htmx web application for e-ink devices
Brooke Kuhlmann
> Terminus is an open source web app built on Ruby, Hanami and htmx for displaying screens on e-ink devices. You'll learn about the design, architecture, and patterns used. Even better, the entire presentation is for e-ink displays (passed to the audience) so you can experience this in real-time!

Brooke's live ePaper setup was one of the coolest demos of the conference.
The whole stack and the way he walked through implementation details made this one extra fun.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/brooke-kuhlmann-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

### Your First Open-Source Contribution
Rachael Wright-Munn
> Is open-source intimidating? Are you nervous about your code being rejected by the maintainers? Or maybe you just don't know where to start. Let's talk about it. Let's talk about the feelings. Let's talk about the practices. Let's get you that first open-source PR.

I got to give this talk at Blue Ridge Ruby, and the response was wonderful.
I love helping people bridge the gap between being great at work and feeling confident contributing in open source.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/rachael-wright-munn-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

{% include gallery.html gallery=page.galleries.rachael_wright_munn_talk %}

### LLM Telemetry as a First-Class Rails Concern
David Paluy
> Your LLM calls deserve the same observability as your database queries. This talk shows how to architect telemetry as a domain concern—not scattered logger calls—so you can review prompts, track costs, and debug production AI features like a proper Rails developer.

David gave a super practical framework for AI instrumentation and evaluation.
The "brisket trial" examples were funny, but the implementation advice was very real and immediately useful.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/david-paluy-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

### Optimize Your Mindset (Without Overclocking)
Christine Seeman
> A playful, research-backed guide to better focus, less stress, and habits that stick. Learn four practical techniques you can use immediately to improve your workday and your life. No woo, just tools that help busy developers thrive.

This talk hit at exactly the right time.
Christine gave practical, grounded advice that felt easy to apply right away and really resonated with the room.

*📺 Recording will be available at [RubyEvents](https://www.rubyevents.org/talks/christine-seeman-blue-ridge-ruby-2026)*

### 5 ways to invest in yourself for the long haul
Thomas Cannon
> Now, more than ever, it's crucial to keep honing your craft. Rather than Herculean efforts every time you need to level up, change jobs, or share your work; let's build a breadcrumb trail! I'll talk about 5 tiny, everyday things that will reap immediate benefits and compound your self-investment.

Thomas closed things out with practical, motivating advice.
The emphasis on tiny daily investments and cumulative payoff was a perfect ending note.
