XO Ruby Atlanta was an intimate Ruby conference that took place in Atlanta, Georgia on September 14, 2025. The single-day event brought together Ruby developers for a collection of inspiring talks, great networking, and the kind of warm community atmosphere that makes Ruby conferences special. The event was organized by @jremsikjr.bsky.social and the team at @beflagrant.com.

### Play with Your Code
**Speaker:** Rachael Wright-Munn (ChaelCodes)

I had the opportunity to present my "Play with Your Code" talk at XO Ruby Atlanta! This talk explores why programming games are more engaging than our day jobs and what lessons we can learn from them to improve our developer experience. We also dive into some fantastic programming games that every developer should try.

My thoughts: It was wonderful to share this talk with the XO Ruby Atlanta audience. The intimate setting made for great interaction and I loved seeing people get excited about discovering new programming games to play with.
